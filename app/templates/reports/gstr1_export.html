About GSTR-1 Export

The generated JSON file contains the following sections per GSTN schema:

  • B2B — Business-to-Business invoices (where billing company has a GSTIN on the check-in record)
  • B2CS — Business-to-Consumer (Small) invoices aggregated by GST rate and place of supply
  • CDNR — Credit notes issued against B2B invoices

Upload the downloaded .json file at return.gst.gov.in under Returns → GSTR-1 → Prepare Online → Upload JSON.
