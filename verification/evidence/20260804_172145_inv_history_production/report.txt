WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANSWER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The database holds one state: the present one. There is no archive
of what it looked like on a past date, so "was this true then"
cannot mean "restore the database and evaluate". Each date is
instead evaluated twice against current data — once with the clock
frozen to that date, once with the clock frozen to today. A
difference between the two is clock-dependence inside a date-scoped
rule. Genuine historical state is available only where a night
audit froze a snapshot, and INV-B03 is the invariant that uses it.
