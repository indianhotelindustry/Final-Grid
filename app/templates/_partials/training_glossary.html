Quick reference — money words mean different things
Revenue = money earned (rooms + extras booked, regardless of cash received)
Settlement = money collected (cash, UPI, card actually in hand)
Receivable = money to be collected (credit owed by guest or company)
Revenue can be high while Settlement is low — that means people booked but haven't paid yet (Receivable).
