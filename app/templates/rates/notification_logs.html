Notification Setup
WhatsApp (UltraMSG)
  1. Sign up at ultramsg.com
  2. Create an instance, connect your WhatsApp Business number
  3. Copy Instance ID and Token
  4. Add to .env:
    ULTRAMSG_INSTANCE=instance12345
    ULTRAMSG_TOKEN=your_token
    FRONTDESK_WHATSAPP=919876543210
Email (Gmail)
  1. Enable 2FA on your Gmail account
  2. Create an App Password: Google Account → Security → App Passwords
  3. Add to .env:
    SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
    SMTP_PORT=587
    SMTP_USER=you@gmail.com
    SMTP_PASS=xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx
    SMTP_FROM=Hotel Name <you@gmail.com>
If ULTRAMSG_INSTANCE or SMTP_HOST are not set, notifications are silently skipped (status = skipped). The booking flow is never affected.
